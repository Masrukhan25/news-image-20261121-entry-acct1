# Saturday 21 November 2026 — the whole day's world news in one picture

One page, thirty-three stories, all from Saturday 21 November 2026.
Up top: Wall Street sinking 3.7% in a deepening AI sell-off, three
Houthi missiles aimed at Riyadh intercepted overnight, and COP31
running into overtime as its draft drops every mention of fossil
fuels. Below them, ten more stories in a grid — Trump's tax threat
over AI data centres abroad, the UK–EU landmark deal, Florida's
floods, Denmark expelling a Russian attaché over a murder plot, a
mathematician's CLAIMED proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, the
Pentagon's eighth UFO file tranche, IndusInd ending its ex-CEO,
Harry Kane's ankle injury, Iraqi forces entering Jurf al-Sakhar,
and GTA VI booking $3.79bn on day one — plus twenty briefs and a
world map with thirty-three numbered pins, one per story.

Nothing on the page is from another day. Every story happened, or
was first reported, on Saturday 21 November 2026; where a count
comes from officials, the poster says so. The red DATE CHECK box
at the bottom names the big Friday stories that were left out on
purpose (the Russian strike on Ukraine and the missile in Poland,
the Gaza drone strike, Nikhil Gupta's sentence) and the two things
that had not happened yet (BepiColombo entering orbit round
Mercury, due that night, and a third COP31 draft).

The image is `entry.png` (1024×1024, PNG). `main.py` — Python 3.13,
standard library only — copies it to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
(default `/output`), checking first that it really is the 1024×1024
PNG this entry ships, and that the copy is byte-identical.
